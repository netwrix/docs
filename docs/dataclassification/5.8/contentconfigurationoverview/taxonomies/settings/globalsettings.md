# Global Settings

The **Content** > **Taxonomies** > **Global Settings** panel lets you update, compare, back up,
export, edit, or delete your taxonomies. See the [Manage Taxonomies](/docs/dataclassification/5.8/contentconfigurationoverview/taxonomies/managetaxonomies/manage.md) topic for additional
information.
