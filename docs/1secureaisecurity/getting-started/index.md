# Getting started

AI Security finds the AI agents and the non-human identities working across the platforms your organization uses. This section explains the terms the AI Security pages use and what 1Secure can do in your environment.

- [Key concepts](/docs/1secureaisecurity/getting-started/key-concepts.md) explains the words the AI Security pages use.
- [What 1Secure can do in your environment](/docs/1secureaisecurity/getting-started/what-1secure-can-do-in-your-environment.md) lists every right 1Secure holds when your Microsoft sources are connected, what each is for, and which you can remove.

When you're ready to set up, check the [Requirements](/docs/1secureaisecurity/requirements/index.md). Then follow [Configuration](/docs/1secureaisecurity/configuration/index.md) to connect your first sources and run your first scan.
