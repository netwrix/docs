# View Your Group Memberships in GroupID

To view the groups that you are a member of, click **Groups** on the left navigation pane and select
**My Groups**. On the My Groups page, click the **My Memberships** tab.

The **My Memberships** tab lists only active groups that you are a member of; expired and deleted
groups aren't displayed.

You can
[Modify Search Directory](/docs/directorymanager/11.0/welcome/group/allgroups/allgroups.md#modify-search-directory)
to search your memberships and add
[Filter All Groups](/docs/directorymanager/11.0/welcome/group/allgroups/allgroups.md#filter-all-groups) by
clicking **My Memberships Grid Filters**. All your memberships matching the filters appear.
