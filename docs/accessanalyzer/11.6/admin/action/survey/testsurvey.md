# Survey: Test Survey

Use this page to test a survey and verify proper configuration.

![Survey Action Module Wizard Test Survey page](/images/accessanalyzer/11.6/admin/action/survey/testsurvey.webp)

The configurable options are:

- Start test – Click to test your survey configuration
- Survey full test – After the survey configuration test passes inspection, test a full survey
  against a single user (for example, your own email account) to verify a survey matches
  design criteria
