# Troubleshooting

You can find troubleshooting content for Endpoint Protector in two places:

- **Knowledge Base**—articles from support cases, grouped by topic, such as deployment and installation, device control, content aware protection, Enforced Encryption, and troubleshooting and errors. See the [Endpoint Protector Knowledge Base](/docs/kb/endpointprotector/endpointprotector).
- **Migration troubleshooting**—known issues and fixes after a migration to the current appliance image. See [Migration Troubleshooting](/docs/endpointprotector/migrationprocedure/troubleshooting.md).
