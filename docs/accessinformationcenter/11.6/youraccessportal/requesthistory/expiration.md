# Access Expiration Email

If only temporary access was granted, after the date expires, the system automatically removes the user
from the resource and sends them an email notification of the removal.

![Access Expired email](/images/accessinformationcenter/11.6/accessrequests/email/expired.webp)

On the
[Request History Page](/docs/accessinformationcenter/11.6/youraccessportal/requesthistory/requesthistory.md)
of the Your Access portal, you can see that the icon in the Expired column has changed and its
tooltip indicates that the access has expired.
