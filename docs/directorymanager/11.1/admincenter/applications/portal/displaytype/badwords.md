# Manage the Bad Words List in a Directory Manager Portal

Use Directory Manager to restrict portal users from saving data that contains words that might be offensive. You can maintain a list of undesired words for a portal. When a user enters a value that contains a word on the list, the portal doesn't save the entry until the user removes or corrects the word.

The bad words check applies to the following:

- Fields with the **Filter Bad Words** option enabled in field properties
- History notes that a user enters for a logged history action

## Add a bad word to the list

1. In Admin Center, select **Applications** in the left pane.

   On the **GroupID Portal** tab, a portal's card displays its info.

2. Click the ellipsis button for a portal and select **Settings**.
3. Select an identity store under **Design Settings** to customize the portal for it.

   All identity stores linked with the portal are listed under **Design Settings**. You can design a different portal for each of these.

4. Click **Bad Words List** in the left pane.
5. On the **Bad Words List** page, click **Add**.
6. In the **Value** box on the **New Bad Word** dialog box, enter a word that you want to prevent users from typing in the portal and click **OK**.

   The **Name** list on the **Bad Words List** page displays the bad word.

7. Ensure the toggle button at the top of the page is enabled to apply the bad words filter to the portal.
8. Click **Save**.

## Update a bad word

1. In Admin Center, select **Applications** in the left pane.

   On the **GroupID Portal** tab, a portal's card displays its info.

2. Click the ellipsis button for a portal and select **Settings**.
3. Select an identity store under **Design Settings** to customize the portal for it.

   All identity stores linked with the portal are listed under **Design Settings**. You can design a different portal for each of these.

4. Click **Bad Words List** in the left pane.
5. On the **Bad Words List** page, click **Edit** for a bad word.
6. On the **Edit Bad Word** dialog box, update the bad word and click **OK**.
7. Click **Save** on the **Bad Words List** page.

## Remove a bad word from the list

1. In Admin Center, select **Applications** in the left pane.

   On the **GroupID Portal** tab, a portal's card displays its info.

2. Click the ellipsis button for a portal and select **Settings**.
3. Select an identity store under **Design Settings** to customize the portal for it.

   All identity stores linked with the portal are listed under **Design Settings**. You can design a different portal for each of these.

4. Click **Bad Words List** in the left pane.
5. On the **Bad Words List** page, click **Remove** for a bad word to remove it.
6. Click **Save**.

## Enable or disable the bad words check

1. In Admin Center, select **Applications** in the left pane.

   On the **GroupID Portal** tab, a portal's card displays its info.

2. Click the ellipsis button for a portal and select **Settings**.
3. Select an identity store under **Design Settings** to customize the portal for it.

   All identity stores linked with the portal are listed under **Design Settings**. You can design a different portal for each of these.

4. Click **Bad Words List** in the left pane.
5. On the **Bad Words List** page, disable the toggle button at the top of the page to disable the bad word check in the portal.

   You can apply the check at any time by enabling the toggle button.

6. Click **Save**.
