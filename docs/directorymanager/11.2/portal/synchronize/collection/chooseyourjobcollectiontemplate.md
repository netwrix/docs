# Choose your Job Template

When creating a job, use Directory Manager Synchronize to create a new job collection based
on your settings and criteria. You can also use multiple templates to directly
create a job collection.

**Follow these steps:**

Step 1 – On Directory Manager portal, select **Synchronize** on left pane.

Step 2 – On the Synchronize portal, click **Create New** and then click **Job Collection.**

Step 3 – On the **Choose Your Job Template** page, enter job collection details and select whether
to use a job group template or create a new job group.

Step 4 – Click **Next Step**.
