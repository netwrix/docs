## 3460 <NSS /> – First Launch

Recommended prerequisite: 2460 <NSS /> – Concepts

The <NSS /> – First Launch course provides an overview of what to expect during your first launch of the product. It covers installation, scanner configuration, Change/Approval Policies, Change Requests, and Change Management Reports, giving you a clear picture of how the product works from setup through day-to-day change tracking.

Estimated length: 45 minutes
