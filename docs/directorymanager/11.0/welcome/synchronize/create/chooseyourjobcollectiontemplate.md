# Choose a Job Collection Template in GroupID

When you create a job, use GroupID Synchronize to create a new job collection based on
your settings and criteria. You can also use multiple templates to directly create a job
collection.

**Follow these steps:**

1. In the GroupID portal, select **Synchronize** in the left pane.
2. In the Synchronize portal, click **Create New** and then click **Job Collection**.
3. On the **Choose Your Job Template** page, enter job collection details and select whether to use
   a job group template or create a new job group.
4. Click **Next Step**.
