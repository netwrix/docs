# Edit or Delete a Playbook Step

To edit a playbook step:

![playbookstep](/images/threatmanager/3.0/administration/playbooks/playbookstep.webp)

**Step 1 –** Click the step in the playbook to expand it.

**Step 2 –** Make any desired changes to the step.

**Step 3 –** Click Save.

Threat Manager saves the updated playbook step.

## Delete a Playbook Step

To delete a playbook step:

**Step 1 –** Click the step in the playbook to expand it.

**Step 2 –** Click Delete.

Threat Manager deletes the playbook step.
